## Outbound person research execution contract

This contract applies only to the canonical Outbound lead research command. Follow the operator's requested scope: a full research covers all seven responsibilities below; a gap closure investigates only requested open fields/persons/roles and preserves accepted evidence. A field-only correction is not a new full research. These instructions clarify person discovery and evidence; they do not authorize additional writes or override the policy-gated writeback contract.

### Search every requested responsibility

First resolve the exact company, country, website and any parent/subsidiary boundaries. Search company + role aliases in small, separate OR groups, first on the official team/management pages and then through Google for LinkedIn/XING profile results. Include the company name/domain and country in queries; do not use one executive-only query for all departments. Repeat ambiguous or empty results with other aliases. The seven responsibilities and relevant titles are:

- Gesamtverantwortung: CEO, Chief Executive Officer, Gründer & Geschäftsführer, Founder & CEO, Executive Director, General Manager, GM, Vorsitzender der Geschäftsführung, Vorstandssprecher, Vorstandsvorsitzender, Managing Director, MD, Managing Partner, Owner & Managing Director, President, Business Unit Director, Country Director, Country Lead, Country Manager, Geschäftsbereichsleiter, Regional Director.
- Finanzen: CFO, Chief Financial Officer, Finanzvorstand, Geschäftsführer Finanzen, Group Finance Director, Kaufmännischer Geschäftsführer, Managing Director Finance, VP Finance, Vice President Finance, Head of Finance mit Prokura, Leiter Finanzen mit Prokura, Commercial Director, Finance Director, Finance Executive, Finanzdirektor, Kaufmännischer Leiter, Leiter Finanz- & Rechnungswesen, Regional Finance Director, Finanzleiter, Senior Finance Manager.
- Einkauf: CPO, Chief Procurement Officer, Einkaufsvorstand, Vorstand Einkauf / Beschaffung, Director Einkauf / Procurement, Director of Procurement, Director of Purchasing, Procurement Director, Bereichsleiter Einkauf, Einkaufsdirektor, Einkaufsgruppenleiter, Einkaufsleiter, Head of Direct Procurement, Head of Procurement, Head of Purchasing, Leiter Beschaffung, Leiter Category Management, Leiter Einkauf, Leiter Materialwirtschaft, Leiter operativer Einkauf, Leiter strategischer Einkauf, Procurement Specialist (Senior), Procurement Team Lead, Purchasing Team Lead, Senior Procurement Manager, SVP Procurement / Purchasing, Teamleiter Beschaffung, Teamleiter Einkauf.

- Operations: COO, Chief Operating Officer, Director of Operations, Geschäftsführer Operations, Global Operations Director, Managing Director Operations, Operativer Geschäftsführer, Regional Operations Director, SVP Operations, Senior Vice President Operations, VP Operations, Vice President Operations, Bereichsleiter Operations, Betriebsleiter Operations, Head of Operations, Leiter Operations, Operations Lead, Senior Operations Manager, Standortleiter, Operations Director.
- Technik: CTO, Chief Technology Officer, Geschäftsführer Technik, Technischer Geschäftsführer, VP Technik, Vice President Technik, Vorstand Technik, Leiter Technik, Bereichsleitung Technik, Betriebsleiter Technik, Entwicklungsleiter, Head of Technology, Technischer Leiter.
- Entwicklung: VP R&D, Vice President Entwicklung, VP Forschung und Entwicklung, Forschung & Entwicklungsleiter, Head of R&D, Research & Development, Leiter F&E, Leiter Forschung & Entwicklung, Leiter F&E / R&D, Leiter Produktentwicklung, Konstruktionsleiter, Entwicklungsleiter.

These are discovery aliases, not proof of responsibility. CPO can mean Chief Product Officer; CTO may cover different responsibilities; a generic manager, purchaser or specialist is not automatically the responsible head. Verify actual function, seniority, current employer and company unit from source content. Prokura remains a separate register fact and does not replace Finanzen, Einkauf or the other responsibilities. One person may cover several responsibilities only when evidence supports each; do not invent seven distinct people.

### Google discovery -> LinkedIn enrichment

For example, search `<company> ("CFO" OR "Finance Director" OR "Leiter Finanzen") site:linkedin.com/in/`, then repeat for each requested responsibility with its own aliases. Use real profile URLs from results after checking name/company identity; never construct a LinkedIn URL from a guessed name. Search snippets are candidates, not proof of current employment. Pass the discovered concrete profile URL/name to the installed Bright Data LinkedIn adapter using its registered input contract, then read its actual result. Bright Data profile enrichment does not replace company/role discovery. If the native person-research plan omits discovery, invoke the supported Google search/registered source explicitly; do not assume discovery is always on. Leadfeeder/CRM may seed candidates but cannot prove an external value alone.

Use the configured Google browser session through the registered adapter. A login challenge, inactive provider account, timeout, missing credential or incomplete source output is blocked/unknown, never evidence that no person exists. Record the actual attempted query/source and failure; use a working independent source when available. Do not bypass account-state or authorization guards, re-register scripts, or start repair cascades in this research.

### Contact data and evidence per person

For each confirmed person collect name, exact original title/function, current company, profile URL, personal company email and available telephone. Bind every value/status/source to the same stable person_key; never inherit another person's evidence. Store URL and a literal quote supporting the particular value, not just the company name. Keep legal representative roles and operational decision makers distinguishable. Preserve conflicting candidates for review rather than selecting an unsupported winner.

When no published personal email is available, derive a bounded set of candidates ONLY from observed email patterns on the verified company domain. Record which known addresses support the pattern. A constructed address is an inference, not a quoted source value: do not fabricate a quote or mark it verified. Submit candidates only through a supported typed candidate/validation path; if none exists, retain them in the research artifact and report the missing validation capability. CTOX's existing daemon validation may validate submitted contact addresses; do not overwrite person_email_validation with no_match merely because the worker cannot perform that check. Inspect the resulting per-person validation receipt. A technical `valid` response proves neither ownership nor current employment by itself; catch-all, unknown and timeout are not confirmed personal contact data. Never send test emails to people. A company switchboard is not a personal direct line.

### Completion and writeback

Keep a coverage table in the retained research artifact for every requested responsibility: queries actually executed, sources/results, matched person_key(s), evidence for current role/company, and outcome found / justified no_match / blocked / conflict / still_open. No_match requires actual completed relevant searches and a documented negative finding; do not manufacture it from a blocked source or an executive-only search. Missing contact data must remain visible even when a person was found. Distinguish researched responsibilities from contacts eligible for a campaign; verified role, contact attribution, validation and explicit contact restrictions must still satisfy the existing release rules.

Before claiming full research success, check all seven responsibility outcomes and all requested per-person fields against persisted writeback/readback receipts. A run that stopped after Geschäftsführer/Prokuristen, has an unsearched department or has blocked/unknown required evidence is incomplete. Write only existing supported fields via business_os.execute_writeback, including person_field_status/person_records where the native contract supports them; keep the coverage table as an artifact rather than inventing new payload fields. A final narrative or a green adapter test is not a successful persisted research. State remaining gaps honestly and do not send a customer mail from this task.
